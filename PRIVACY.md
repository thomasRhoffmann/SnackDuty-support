# SnackDuty Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

**SnackDuty: Team Planner** (“SnackDuty”) is developed by Thomas Hoffmann. This policy describes how the app handles information.

## Summary

SnackDuty is designed to keep your team data **on your iPhone only**. We do not operate servers that store your seasons, rosters, or schedules. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Information you enter in the app

You may enter information such as:
- Season and team names
- Household and player names
- Phone numbers (for SMS compose)
- Allergy notes
- Event dates, titles, and locations

This information is stored **locally on your device** using Apple’s SwiftData framework. It is not transmitted to us.

## Information we do not collect

SnackDuty does **not**:
- Require an account or login
- Upload your roster or schedule to our servers
- Use analytics or advertising SDKs
- Track you across other apps or websites

## When data leaves your device

Data only leaves your device when **you choose** to:
- Share a PDF via the system share sheet
- Send an SMS or message through the system Messages app (you control recipients and content)
- Back up your iPhone via iCloud or a computer backup (handled by Apple, not SnackDuty)

We do not receive copies of that shared content.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, SnackDuty schedules **local notifications** on your device. Notification permission is requested by iOS. We do not send push notifications from a server.

## Children

SnackDuty is intended for adult coordinators managing youth team activities. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through our systems because we do not collect data on our servers.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
