# SnackDuty Support

**SnackDuty: Team Planner** is an iPhone app for youth sports team coordinators. It helps you build a snack rotation schedule, export a PDF for parents, and send SMS reminders — with all data stored locally on your device.

This repository is for **support and privacy information only**. The app source code is not published here.

## Get help

**[Open an issue](https://github.com/thomasRhoffmann/snackduty-support/issues)** — best for bug reports, feature requests, or how-to questions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my team data stored in the cloud?

No. Seasons, rosters, events, and assignments are stored **only on your iPhone** using Apple’s on-device storage. SnackDuty does not require an account and does not sync to our servers.

### How do I share the schedule with others?

Use **Export** in the app to generate a PDF, then share via Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or any app in the share sheet. You can also send a pre-filled SMS to the assigned household when SMS is available on your device.

### Why doesn’t SMS work in the Simulator?

The iOS Simulator cannot use the system SMS composer. On Simulator, SnackDuty copies the message to your clipboard and opens Messages. On a physical iPhone with SMS, the app opens the standard Messages composer.

### How do reminders work?

Reminders are **local notifications** on your device (e.g. 24 hours and 2 hours before an event). They are for the coordinator using the app, not push notifications sent from a server. Enable them in the app and allow notifications when iOS prompts you.

### Can I use SnackDuty for multiple teams?

Yes. Create a separate **season** per team or term. Archive old seasons when you’re done.
